TRACKING GENES & REPRODUCTION

Tracking Genes and Reproduction topics include: sexual reproduction, asexual reproduction, fertilisation, mitosis, meiosis, variation and mutation. This topic is important becase it explains how life adapts and evolves by undersdtanding how genetic material is passed down and expressed. It's also important so that scientists can advance medicine and human health. An example of this are cancer treatments that are caused by genetic mutations.
