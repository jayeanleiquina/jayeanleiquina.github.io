POPULATION GENETICS

Population Genetics topics include: genetic variation, natural selection, population, evolution, adaptaion and migration. This topic is important because it explains how evolutionary mechanisms like natural selection change gene and allele frequencies over time. It helps scientists measure genetic diversity and population sizes, which are important to save and conserve endangered species from extinction.
