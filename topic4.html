GENETIC TECHNOLOGY

Genetic Techonology topics include: DNA sequencing, genetic tracking, genetic profiling and genetic screening. This topic is important because it directly improves biological functions at the DNA level. It advances medical treatments such as gene therapy, which treats or correct genetic disorders by replacing genes. It also enhances agricultural crops because they can develop plant varities that are resistant to pests and droughts.
