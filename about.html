About Page

Year 10 Science to Level 1 Science is a huge jump for most highschoolers. In junior school, biology introduces the basic building blocks of science. Tests are mostly multiple choice, and most can get away with not studying. Level 1 is much different, tests are open-ended, and the content is much deeper. Test answers are very specific, and you can lose marks by not including a definition, or if your explanation is different than the model answer. This quiz is designed for those who struggle, who are willing to put in the work in order to achieve those high marks. This website is designed by a student, for students.
