GENETIC VARIATION

Genetic Variation topics include: gene, organism, allele, dna, chromosome, genotype, phenotype, homozygous and heterozygous. This topic is important because it is the basic building block of genetics. It is important to understand the basic concepts of genetics in order to understand more complex topics such as evolution and natural selection. Understanding these definitions helps scientists to diagnose inherited diseases and develop new treatments.
