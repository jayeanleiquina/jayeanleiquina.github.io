GENETIC VARIATION

Genetic Variation topics include: gene, organism, allele, dna, chromosome, genotype, phenotype, homozygous and heterozygous. Gene: A section of DNA that codes for a protein/trait. Organism: An individual, animal, plant or single-celled life form. Allele: An alternative form of a gene. DNA: Deoxyribonucleic acid, which carries the genetic information of an organism. It is made up of two strands of repeating nucleotides (sugar, phosphate and a base)
